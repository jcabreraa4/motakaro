Module 1 - Fundamentals of a Modern B2B GTM Strategy

With the democratization of access to information, B2B buyer behavior has changed radically. Today's buyers are taking control of their buying process, relying on new sources of information and rejecting traditional cold sales methods.

Today's B2B Buyer Characteristics

Is fully autonomous: Researches on their own without following a linear path, forms their own opinions and delays contact with suppliers until late in the decision process.

Learns in the “dark social”: Is informed through social networks, communities, forums, podcasts and channels untraceable by traditional attribution tools.

Shuns commercial pressure: Rejects cold approaches and invasive tactics, preferring to make contact on their own terms when they feel it is necessary.

Seeks closeness and integrity: Expects human, close and clear communication regarding product or service features, pricing, benefits and limitations.

"In a modern strategy, the sale is made by helping the buyer make the best logical decision."

Despite the transformation of the buyer, most Go-To-Market strategies in the B2B environment are still anchored in outdated models that do not respond to this new reality.

Traditional vs Modern Strategies

There is a significant gap between how the customer buys and how companies sell, generating inefficiencies that translate into lost opportunities, time and resources. As a result, customer acquisition efforts become costly, inefficient and, in many cases, unsustainable.To adapt to today's buyer, companies need to evolve to a GTM strategy focused on market research, demand generation through education and an intent-based sales approach.

Module 2 - Market Research and Narrative Development

Market research is the foundation of every modern GTM strategy: It helps us to get to know our ideal customer profile (ICP) in depth, understanding their current context, the challenges they face and the solutions they are considering.This knowledge allows us to influence their buying journey through content that reflects their reality, generates resonance and naturally educates them towards our service.

"Without market research, any attempt to connect with the customer will be a gamble."

Market Research

Interviews with customers: Why did they choose us? What do they value about our service?

Conversations with prospects: What concerns them the most? What objections do they have?

Observation in communities and forums: What questions do they ask? What terms do they use?

Collaboration with sales and CS: What patterns are they seeing on a day-to-day basis?

Once all this market information has been collected and analyzed, we must transform these insights into a persuasive narrative that connects with our ICP in a natural way.

Narrative Development

Problem articulation: Formulate the problem your ideal customer is suffering from, using their own language and in a way that resonates with them.

Value differentiation: Establish what makes your solution unique without focusing on technical features, but rather by showing how it solves the problem in a logical way.

Own category creation: Transform the unique differentiation into a market space of its own, making price competition irrelevant.

Narrative structure: Develop a coherent and logical narrative that guides your ICP from recognizing the problem to the ideal solution.

This narrative will serve as the fundamental strategic framework upon which we will build all of our demand generation content.

Module 3 - Content Creation that Generates Demand

It's time to transform our narrative into content that connects with our ICP and generates qualified demand, with video being the best format to achieve this goal as efficiently as possible.

"The way you present and convey your message is as important as the message itself."

Video as a Strategic Format

Brand humanization: It allows showing expressions and personalities behind the company.

Conveying ideas: Combines visual and auditory elements for a more complete understanding.

Less saturation: While written content abounds, there is less competition in video format in B2B.

Platforms prioritization: Platforms like LinkedIn favor video content in their algorithms.

Trust building: Buyers can “meet” the people behind the solution before any direct interaction.

Awareness vs Retargeting Content

For an effective strategy, we only need to focus on two types of content according to level of awareness: Awareness content, for cold profiles that don't know us, and retargeting content, for profiles that have shown signs of purchase intentionality.

Once the type of content we need has been defined, it is essential to establish a systematic and efficient process for its production. The key is to follow a methodology that allows us to generate authentic content in a consistent and scalable way.

For that purpose, we have been refining our structured interviews, monthly sessions with the founder or C-levels in a conversational but guided format.

Structured Interviews

Strategic preparation: Questions are designed to extract content that reinforces the narrative.

Humanized format: Relaxed atmosphere favors authenticity and prevents rehearsed responses.

Multiple clip editing: Extraction of 30-90 second excerpts that function as stand-alone pieces.

Creative rotation: Producing multiple variants allows to test which generate greater resonance.

Efficiency and scalability: It is repeatable, can evolve over time and contains content for weeks.

This methodology maximizes production efficiency (one hour of interviewing generates content for weeks), ensures the authenticity sought by today's B2B buyers, and provides varied content that can be used strategically depending on the stage of the buying journey.

Module 4 - Educating Prospects through LinkedIn Ads

LinkedIn ads are an extraordinary way to boost and accelerate the effects of your B2B demand generation strategy, being cheaper than traditionally believed when executed in the right way.

LinkedIn Ads Ecosystem

Native professional ecosystem: LinkedIn is the platform where B2B professionals go specifically to educate themselves and solve business problems, which means your ads appear where your ICP is professionally minded and active.

Increased targeting capabilities: The platform offers unparalleled B2B filters such as exact job title, seniority level, company size and specific industry, allowing you to target messages exclusively to your ideal customer.

Customized lists and audiences: LinkedIn allows you to upload specific lists of companies and contacts for effective ABM campaigns, in addition to retargeting visitors to your website or people who have interacted with your content.

Manual control and personalization: While other platforms are moving towards automated campaigns, LinkedIn maintains full manual control options over targeting, budgets and creative, allowing for greater optimization capabilities.

Leveraging these unique advantages of LinkedIn, we can implement an ad strategy that respects the natural B2B buyer's journey, with educational content for cold audiences and evolving to more targeted messaging as we detect signs of interest.

Thought Leader Ads for Awareness

Start distributing multiple pieces of content to C-level profiles and monitor their natural performance with no upfront investment.

We keep 10-15 creatives in constant rotation, optimizing budget towards top performers and avoiding ad fatigue.

Visibility and engagement metrics are analyzed to detect which messages resonate best with the organic audience.

The best performing organic content is boosted through Thought Leader Ads, preserving the natural look and feel of a personal publication.

Once the outreach strategy is implemented, the real value of LinkedIn Ads is not in the immediate conversion, but in its ability to generate and identify genuine signals of interest that allow us to qualify prospects for later stages of the funnel.

Intentionality Signals Capture

Video viewing: We identify users who show real interest by watching at least 50% of a video.

Website visitors: We capture those who visit your site after engaging with LinkedIn content.

Qualitative interactions: We track all kind of interactions that reveal specific interests.

Qualification filter: We apply an additional filter to prioritize profiles that match your ICP.

These intent signals are extremely valuable, as they identify prospects that not only match our ICP, but also have demonstrated active interest. Now is the time to nurture these relationships through an educational retargeting strategy.

Educational Retargeting Campaigns

Strategic duration: We implement 60-120 day campaigns that respect the long decision cycles typical of the B2B environment.

Content sequencing: We progress from core messages about the problem to specific solutions and differentiation.

Mix of formats: We alternate customer testimonials, educational content and responses to common objections to progressively build trust.

Controlled exposure: We maintain a frequency of 2-3 weekly impacts, enough to maintain presence without generating a sense of harassment.

Throughout the retargeting process, we build trust and educate our prospects. When they have consumed enough content and have shown continued interest, it is the ideal time to facilitate conversion to a customer.

🙋🏻‍♂ Module 5 - Approach triggered by Buying Intent Signals

Many prospects, after gaining enough trust during educational retargeting, take the initiative on their own and decide to contact the sales team. This is the ideal scenario, as they arrive fully qualified and ready to buy.If, after interacting and showing intent to purchase, the potential customer has not taken steps to make the purchase, it is time to take action and establish contact.

Sales Contact Methods

Lead Gen Form Ads: For those prospects that show clear signs of interest but have not taken the step to contact directly, we implement campaigns with native LinkedIn forms. These have minimal friction by auto-completing fields with professional profile information, significantly increasing conversion rates.

SDRs team: Alternatively, we can redirect prospects to the sales team for a personalized approach. When contacted, SDRs will no longer be perceived as "cold salespeople", but as representatives of a brand with which you are already familiar.

Email campaign: As a complement or alternative, we can integrate these prospects into strategically designed email campaigns. Thanks to the familiarity generated through content on LinkedIn, open and response rates will increase significantly.

In any case, the key is timing: the commercial contact comes when the prospect already knows us, trusts our expertise and is educated about the problem we solve, eliminating the main barrier of traditional cold outbound.
